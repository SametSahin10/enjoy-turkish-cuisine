**Baklava** is Turkey's most famous sweet: dozens of layers of gossamer-thin filo
pastry brushed with butter, packed with finely ground **pistachios** (or walnuts),
baked until shatteringly crisp, and finished with a light sugar syrup.

## How it's eaten

Baklava is rich, so it's served in small diamond-cut pieces with strong coffee or
tea to balance the sweetness. The very best — from **Gaziantep** — is bright green
inside from premium Antep pistachios.

## Tips for travelers

Look for **"fıstıklı"** (pistachio) baklava for the classic experience. It travels
well and makes a beloved edible souvenir.
