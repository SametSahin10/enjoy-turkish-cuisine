İskender kebab is one of the proudest dishes of the city of **Bursa**, in
northwestern Turkey. It takes thin slices of seasoned lamb or beef döner and lays
them over bite-sized pieces of pide (flatbread), then smothers everything in a
rich tomato sauce. Just before serving, the waiter pours **foaming hot butter**
over the plate at your table — a small piece of theater that makes the dish
unforgettable.

## How it's eaten

A generous dollop of cool, tangy yogurt sits alongside the hot, buttery meat. The
contrast between the warm sauce and the cool yogurt is the whole point. Mix a
little of each into every bite.

## Tips for travelers

İskender is hearty — usually a meal on its own. It's served at lunch and dinner,
and a "bir buçuk" (one-and-a-half portion) is common for bigger appetites.
