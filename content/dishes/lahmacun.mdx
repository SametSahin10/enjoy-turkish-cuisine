Often called "Turkish pizza" by visitors, though locals will gently correct you,
**lahmacun** is a wafer-thin round of dough baked with a thin layer of minced meat
seasoned with peppers, tomato, garlic, and parsley.

## How it's eaten

Don't eat it flat. Pile on fresh parsley and onion, squeeze over plenty of
**lemon**, then roll it up like a wrap and eat with your hands.

## Tips for travelers

It's light, cheap, and fast, so many people eat two. Pair it with a glass of
**ayran** (a salty yogurt drink) for the full experience.
