**Döner** — from the Turkish word for "to turn" — is the rotating tower of
marinated meat you'll see glistening in shop windows across Turkey. As the outer
layer roasts, the cook shaves off thin, crispy-edged slices to order.

## Ways to order it

- **Dürüm** — wrapped in a thin lavash flatbread with vegetables.
- **Ekmek arası** — stuffed into bread like a sandwich.
- **Porsiyon** — a plate with rice or fries, no bread.

## Tips for travelers

Döner is the perfect cheap, fast, and reliable meal between sightseeing stops.
Chicken (tavuk) and beef/lamb (et) are the usual choices.
