**Künefe** is a warm dessert built from **kadayıf** — fine shredded pastry — packed
around a layer of mild, unsalted cheese, then baked until crisp and golden and
doused in sugar syrup. It arrives at your table piping hot, often crowned with
ground **pistachio**.

## How it's eaten

Eat it immediately, while the cheese inside is still molten and stretchy. A scoop
of kaymak (clotted cream) or ice cream on the side is common.

## Tips for travelers

Künefe is a shared, celebratory dessert — order it after a meal and pair it with
**Turkish tea** or coffee.
